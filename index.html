How we find your window

We compare consecutive daylight hours between 6 AM and 8 PM, using your outing duration. Lower rain chance, lighter wind, and comfortable apparent temperatures score better. Thunderstorm hours are excluded. Times that have already passed are excluded from today. This is a forecast-based planning aid; check trail conditions and local alerts before heading out.
